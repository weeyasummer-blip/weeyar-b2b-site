How Weeyar works

Weeyar operates as a supply-chain and project-coordination partner. We compare suitable market-ready products and manufacturing capabilities, coordinate samples and packaging, follow project specifications, and support export delivery. We do not describe every partner facility, production line or certificate as company-owned.

Facility qualifications, testing documents and certificates are confirmed according to the selected product, manufacturing partner and destination market. Documentation is provided for eligible projects where available.

Cooperation routes

Ready stock

Suitable for faster product evaluation and smaller trial orders. Current inventory, packaging and dispatch timing must be confirmed before ordering.

Private label

Uses a selected existing formula with brand-specific labels or cartons. MOQ depends on formula availability and packaging requirements.

OEM / ODM

Supports projects requiring formula development, packaging coordination or additional customization. Sampling, MOQ and lead time are assessed for the specific brief.

Product formats

Project formats may include gummies, capsules and probiotics, liquid drops, powders and functional formats, sprays and oral strips. Current availability and customization differ by product.

What buyers should provide

For a useful recommendation or quotation, share the destination market, reference product or formula direction, preferred dosage format, packaging needs, estimated quantity and target timing. Weeyar then confirms feasible routes, samples, MOQ, packaging and available documentation.

Accuracy and claims

Website content is intended for B2B cooperation and sourcing evaluation. Final formula, ingredients, serving size, claims, packaging, specifications, documentation and compliance requirements are confirmed for the selected product and destination market. No website content should be interpreted as medical advice or a disease-treatment claim.
