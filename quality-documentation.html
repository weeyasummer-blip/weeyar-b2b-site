DOCUMENT REQUEST AREAS

What Buyers Can Ask Us to Coordinate

These are request categories, not blanket promises that every document applies to every product. We confirm relevance and availability before sharing project materials.

01

Product Specification

Current dosage format, ingredient declaration, serving presentation and other product-specific details available for the selected commercial option.

02

Quality & Test Documents

Available COA, test-report or batch-document information is checked according to the product, sample or applicable production lot.

03

Partner Qualifications

Relevant facility qualification or certification details may be reviewed after the suitable manufacturing partner and required scope are identified.

04

Packaging Information

Container, count, label, carton and protective-packaging details are confirmed for the selected product and branding route.

05

Label & Market Inputs

Ingredient statements, intended claims and label requirements require review against the destination market and final confirmed specification.

06

Export Coordination

Commercial and shipping documents are coordinated according to the order, delivery route and destination requirements.

Important: Weeyar works through a Partner Manufacturing Network. Facility certificates belong to the selected manufacturing partner; Weeyar does not present every facility, production line or certificate as company-owned.

  • Document or certificate name
  • Actual certificate holder
  • Issuing body and applicable scope
  • Issue and validity dates where relevant
  • A clear manufacturing-partner attribution

What may be protected

  • Personal signatures and contact details

  • Full files intended only for qualified project review
BUYER FAQ

Questions About Supplement Documentation

Which documents can I request?

You may ask about product specifications, available quality or test documents, packaging information and applicable manufacturing-partner qualification details. The available set depends on the product and project.

Are manufacturing certificates held by Weeyar?

Weeyar is a supply-chain and project-coordination partner. Facility certificates are held by the selected manufacturing partner, and their validity and scope must be confirmed for the relevant product and destination market.

Can I receive a COA before placing an order?

Representative or currently available documentation may be reviewed for eligible projects. Batch-specific documents relate to the applicable production lot and are confirmed according to the project stage.

Why do you ask for my target market?

Document relevance, labeling and product requirements vary by country or region. Knowing the destination helps us identify the right product and manufacturing route.
