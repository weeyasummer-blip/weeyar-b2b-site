B2B PROJECT PLANNING

Plan a Multivitamin Gummy Project

Multivitamin gummies can vary in vitamin selection, ingredient amounts, serving size, flavor and market requirements. Buyers should review the complete specification rather than relying only on the front-label product name.

What to confirm before requesting samples

  • Complete ingredient specification and amounts per serving
  • Serving size, gummy weight, flavor, color and texture
  • Bottle count, closure, label area, carton and case packing
  • Destination-market label, claim and documentation requirements

What determines MOQ and lead time?

MOQ and timing depend on the selected formula batch, flavor and gummy production requirements, bottle availability, printing quantities and artwork approval. Review our supplement MOQ guide.
