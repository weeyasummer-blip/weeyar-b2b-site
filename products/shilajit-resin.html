Define the Resin Before Comparing Quotations

“Shilajit resin” is a product-format name, not a complete purchasing specification. A useful comparison starts with the sourcing route and the evidence attached to the selected commercial option.

EXISTING OPTION

Current Product Reference

Use this route when the priority is evaluating an available material and packaging direction with limited changes.

  • Request the current commercial specification
  • Confirm what the sample represents
  • Share quantity and destination market
BRANDED ROUTE

Private Label

Use this route when an existing product direction is suitable and branded packaging is the main requirement.

  • Confirm jar, fill and sealing options
  • Prepare brand artwork and required label content
  • Check printed-component quantities before design
PROJECT REVIEW

Custom Specification

Use this route when material attributes, fill, packaging or document requirements need separate review.

  • Provide a written target specification
  • Identify required tests and market needs
  • Confirm feasibility before samples and quotation
