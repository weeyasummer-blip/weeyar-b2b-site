Creatine monohydrate

The named creatine ingredient in the supplied reference. Ask for the specification and declared amount per serving when comparing product options.

Vitamin C and vitamin B12

Both vitamins are listed in the supplied materials; vitamin B12 is identified as cyanocobalamin. Their listed presence does not establish the amount per serving.

L-taurine and betaine

The reference also identifies L-taurine and betaine anhydrous. Confirm the complete blend and ingredient declaration for your selected product.

Supporting ingredients already listed in the reference include citric acid, sodium citrate and vegetable oil. Confirm the complete ingredient list, allergens, gummy base and sweeteners during product review.

Serving directions & storage

The bottle contains 80 gummies. This is the pack count, not a serving recommendation. Follow the directions on the selected product label; request the current label if you need to review daily quantity or timing before ordering.

Keep sealed in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight. The listed shelf life is 24 months; confirm the batch dates on the supplied product.

Can I order a few bottles with my own label?Custom label or packaging orders start at 500 bottles. Ready-stock mixed orders use existing-brand packaging and start at 20 bottles total, with at least 3 per product. Sample quantities, packaging, charges and shipping are confirmed separately.

What should buyers confirm first?Confirm the actual formula, creatine amount and serving presentation, gummy system, packaging, target market and applicable documents.

Can buyers request samples?Yes. Ask us to confirm sample quantity, packaging, charges and shipping. Small sample requests do not qualify for a custom-branded production order; custom orders start at 500 bottles.
