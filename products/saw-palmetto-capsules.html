Samples, Branding & Delivery

For bottled ready-stock products in existing-brand packaging, mixed orders start at 20 bottles total, with at least 3 per product. Bottle-label customization starts at 500 bottles. Pouches, new containers, other packaging and custom formulas have separately confirmed minimums.

Sample quantities, charges and freight are confirmed separately. Ready-stock orders ship within 5–7 days after payment. Customized orders ship 15–20 days after sample approval; shipping transit time is additional.
