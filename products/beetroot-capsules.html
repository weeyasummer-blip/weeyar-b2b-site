Samples, Branding & Delivery

Bottled ready-stock mixed orders start at 20 bottles total, at least 3 per product, subject to current availability. Bottle-label customization starts at 500 bottles. Custom formulas, containers and cartons have separately confirmed minimums.

Sample quantities, charges and freight are quoted separately. Ready stock dispatches 5–7 days after payment; customized orders dispatch 15–20 days after sample approval. Transit time is additional.

Weeyar coordinates sourcing through a partner manufacturing network. The current formula, packaging and available documentation are reviewed for the selected product.
