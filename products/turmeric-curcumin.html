Samples & sourcing questions

What should I send for a quote?

Share your destination country, approximate quantity and whether you need an existing product or your own label. We will confirm suitable options and available specifications.

Can I review a sample?

Ask what the sample represents and compare it with the proposed specification. Sample availability, fees and shipping are confirmed for the selected option and destination.

What is the minimum order?

For confirmed bottled stock, mixed orders start at 20 bottles total with at least 3 per product. Bottle-label projects start at 500 bottles. Formula changes and other formats require a separate minimum-order confirmation.
