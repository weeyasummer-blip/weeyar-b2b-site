B2B PRODUCT DECISION

Define the Product Before Comparing Quotations

“Creatine hydration powder” is a commercial concept, not a complete specification. A useful comparison starts with the product route and the details that must be confirmed.

EXISTING OPTION

Ready-Stock Reference

Use this route when the priority is evaluating an available product direction with limited changes.

  • Confirm the current specification and packaging
  • Check whether the sample represents current stock
  • Share quantity and destination market
BRANDED ROUTE

Private Label

Use this route when an existing product direction is suitable and branded packaging is the main requirement.

  • Confirm pouch size and available packaging
  • Prepare brand artwork and required label content
  • Check packaging quantities before final design
PROJECT REVIEW

Custom Development

Use this route when the ingredient profile, flavor, sweetener system or serving format needs review.

  • Provide a written target specification
  • Identify required and optional product attributes
  • Confirm feasibility before samples and quotation
BUYER BRIEF

What Should a Buyer Confirm?

A product name alone does not establish the formula. Ask which elements are already fixed, which can be adjusted and which require a separate development project.
