5-Step OEM Journey

How We Take Your Brand From Idea to Shelf

A clear, structured process — whether you need ready-stock private label or fully custom OEM/ODM development.

1

Formula R&D

We start with your vision — target market, desired benefits, and format preferences. Our R&D team develops or adapts formulas using clinically-backed ingredients. Choose from 500+ mature formulas or commission a fully custom blend. All formulations are pre-vetted for CPNP, Halal, and GPSR compliance.

2

Branding & Design

Your brand, your identity. Our in-house design team creates custom labels, packaging, and product presentation tailored to your target market. We handle everything from bottle design and pouch artwork to barcode generation and regulatory labeling — ensuring your product looks professional and sells on any shelf.

3

Sample Approval

Before mass production begins, you receive physical samples for evaluation. Test the taste, texture, packaging quality, and overall product experience. Request adjustments — we iterate until you are 100% satisfied. Sample turnaround is typically 3–5 business days for ready-stock formulas.

4

Quality Production

Once samples are approved, we move into full-scale production in our GMPC and HACCP-certified facility. Every batch undergoes rigorous quality control — including potency verification, microbial testing, and stability checks. Production lead times are transparently communicated and consistently met.

5

Global Logistics

We coordinate international shipping to your destination — whether it's FCL to Los Angeles, LCL to Rotterdam, or air freight to Dubai. Our logistics team handles customs documentation, certificates of origin, and compliance paperwork so your products arrive on time and ready for distribution.
