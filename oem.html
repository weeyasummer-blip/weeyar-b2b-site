OEM / ODM FAQ

Planning a Supplement Project

Answers to common questions about project routes, samples, packaging and quotations.

Ready stock, private label or custom formula?

Ready stock uses available products for a faster start. Private label applies your branding to a selected formula. A custom formula is developed around your product brief. Compare private label and custom formula in detail.

What determines the MOQ?

For bottled products, ready-stock mixed orders start at 20 bottles in total, with a minimum of 3 bottles per product and customized orders start at 500 bottles. For pouches, sachets and other packaging formats, please confirm the minimum order quantity with us. Ready-stock orders ship within 5–7 days after payment. Customized orders ship 15–20 days after sample approval. Shipping transit time is additional.

Can I request samples before production?

Yes, sample arrangements are part of the project process. The route and timing depend on whether you choose an existing formula or custom development.

Do you support custom packaging?

Explore labels and packaging concepts with us. Specific containers, sachets, cartons and gift sets require confirmation of availability, product compatibility and project requirements. See the packaging options.

What information is needed for a quote?

Share your target market, preferred product or formula, packaging needs and estimated quantity. You can use our quote form or WhatsApp.

How should I evaluate a manufacturer?

Review product fit, quality process, sample handling, packaging, commercial terms and communication. Read our B2B manufacturer checklist.
