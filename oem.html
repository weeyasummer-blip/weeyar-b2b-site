OEM / ODM FAQ

Planning a Supplement Project

Answers to common questions about project routes, samples, packaging and quotations.

Ready stock, private label or custom formula?

Ready stock uses available products for a faster start. Private label applies your branding to a selected formula. A custom formula is developed around your product brief.

What determines the MOQ?

MOQ depends on the formula, dosage format, packaging choice and estimated quantity. Exact terms are confirmed after reviewing your project.

Can I request samples before production?

Yes, sample arrangements are part of the project process. The route and timing depend on whether you choose an existing formula or custom development.

Do you support custom packaging?

Packaging support can include labels, bottles, jars, pouches, sachets and cartons. See the packaging options.

What information is needed for a quote?

Share your target market, preferred product or formula, packaging needs and estimated quantity. You can use our quote form or WhatsApp.

How should I evaluate a manufacturer?

Review product fit, quality process, sample handling, packaging, commercial terms and communication. Read our B2B manufacturer checklist.
