Four Decisions Before a Project Quote

  1. Choose the product: share a product link or preferred format; mark any required formula attributes.
  2. Define the packaging: state whether you need existing packaging, your label or a separately reviewed packaging design.
  3. Share market and quantity: provide the destination country and an estimated order size if known. A final quantity is not required to start a conversation.
  4. Review the proposed scope: request available specifications, sample options, packaging details and applicable documents before accepting the quotation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I start without a final formula or order quantity?

Yes. Share a product reference or format and your destination market. We can discuss the missing specification and quantity details before recommending a project route.

Can my logo be added to the bottle and carton?

Private-label projects can generally support brand-specific labels and cartons. Available materials, printing methods and minimum quantities are confirmed for the selected product.

Can I request samples and quality documents?

Sample availability and timing depend on the product and requested changes. Available specifications and batch documents are confirmed for the selected product and supply route; not every document is available for every project.

Does Weeyar own the manufacturing facility?

Weeyar is a dietary supplement supply-chain partner working through a Partner Manufacturing Network. We coordinate suitable manufacturing, packaging, sampling and available documentation; facility qualifications belong to the selected partner.
