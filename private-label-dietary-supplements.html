Available supplement formats

What buyers should send for a useful quotation

  • Target country or sales market
  • Product reference, formula direction or preferred format
  • Estimated order quantity and launch timing
  • Preferred bottle, pouch, carton or label direction
  • Required samples and available reference artwork
  • Any destination-market document requirements already identified

Sampling, packaging and project workflow

A practical project normally moves through requirement review, product or formula selection, sample confirmation, packaging and artwork approval, production coordination, batch documentation and export preparation. The exact sequence depends on whether the project uses ready stock, private label or custom development.

For a detailed pre-order review, use our B2B supplement order checklist. Buyers comparing project routes can also read Private Label vs. Custom Formula.

Partner manufacturing and quality coordination

Weeyar operates as a dietary supplement supply-chain partner. We coordinate suitable partner manufacturing capabilities, packaging resources, sampling, quality documentation and export support according to the selected product and destination market. This positioning gives buyers a single project contact without implying that every facility or production line is owned by Weeyar.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Sample availability and timing depend on the product, formula and required adjustments.

Can my logo be added to the bottle and carton?

Private-label projects can generally support brand-specific labels and cartons. Available materials, printing methods and minimum quantities are confirmed for the selected product.

Can you provide COA and batch documents?

Available specifications and batch documents are confirmed against the selected product, production route and destination-market requirements before the order is finalized.
