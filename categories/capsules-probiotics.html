COMMON BUYER QUESTIONS

Capsule & Probiotic Sourcing Answers

Can an existing probiotic use my brand?Private-label packaging can be discussed for selected existing options. The route depends on availability, branding scope and quantity.
Is a species name a complete specification?Not necessarily. Ask for the identification and declared-count information that applies to the selected commercial formula.
What should a sample represent?Confirm whether it represents current stock, an existing formula in reference packaging or a custom-development version.
Why is storage part of the quotation?Storage and packaging direction should match the selected product specification and intended distribution conditions.
