FORMAT SELECTION

Start With the Buyer Brief

Powders, oral strips and sprays create different serving and packaging experiences. Before selecting a product, define what the buyer and destination market actually require.

MIXABLE FORMAT

Powders

Suitable when the concept depends on a prepared drink, scoop, sachet or other measured powder presentation.

  • Confirm formula and serving direction
  • Review flavor, sweetener and mixing expectations
  • Choose pouch, jar or sachet direction
PORTABLE FORMAT

Oral Strips

Useful when portability and an individually presented serving format are central to the product concept.

  • Confirm strip specification and unit presentation
  • Review sachet and retail-box requirements
  • Check market and labeling needs
COMPACT DELIVERY

Sprays

Useful for brands evaluating a compact liquid presentation with spray-bottle and carton packaging.

  • Confirm bottle, pump and fill presentation
  • Review flavor and serving instructions
  • Match packaging to the project route

COMMON BUYER QUESTIONS

Answers Before You Request Options

How should buyers choose a format?Compare the target customer, serving experience, formula direction, packaging, market and quantity. Popularity alone does not determine the best format.
Can these products use private-label packaging?Private-label options can be discussed for selected products. Branding scope and packaging quantities are confirmed by project.
What should a powder quotation brief include?Include the market, positioning, required attributes, flavor direction, serving format, packaging, quantity and required documents.
Where can buyers compare hydration concepts?Review the Creatine Hydration Powder and Lemonade Electrolyte Drink Mix pages, then use the sourcing guide to prepare questions.
