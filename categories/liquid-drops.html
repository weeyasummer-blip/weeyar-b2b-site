What Goes Into a Private Label Liquid Supplement Quote?

Compare quotations using the same formula, bottle volume, quantity and destination. A unit price alone does not show the full project cost.

Product & Quantity

Confirm the ingredient specification, serving declaration, fill volume and number of bottles. Bottled ready-stock mixed orders start at 20 bottles total, at least 3 per product, in existing-brand packaging; current stock is confirmed separately.

Your Label & Packaging

Bottle-label customization starts at 500 bottles. Review label artwork, bottle and dropper compatibility, seals and optional cartons. Custom formulas, new containers and other packaging have separately confirmed minimums and costs.

Samples & Delivery

Request sample quantity, fees and freight separately. Include your destination country and postal code so shipping can be quoted for the selected packing configuration.
