Private Label Creatine Gummies: Samples, Serving & Powder Comparison

Buyer questions to settle before comparing samples, packaging and quotations.

Published November 4, 2026 · B2B procurement guide

Start with the specification: compare the declared creatine ingredient and amount per serving, complete formula, sample version, packaging and commercial route. An 80-count gummy bottle and a powder pack are different presentations; compare quotations on the same agreed specification.

1. Read pack count and serving information separately

The supplied Docteat creatine gummy reference contains 80 gummies per bottle. This does not establish gummies per serving, daily quantity or creatine amount. Ask for the current label and specification for the option you intend to order.

  • Which creatine ingredient is declared?
  • What is the declared amount per serving, and how many gummies make a serving?
  • Is a stated amount for creatine itself or a wider ingredient blend?
  • Does the specification identify the same product and sample version?

Review these details before comparing per-bottle prices. Serving and ingredient amounts for private-label projects must be confirmed rather than copied from reference artwork.

The creatine hydration powder page is a separate product route. Do not assume its blend or serving is equivalent to the gummy reference. This is a purchasing comparison, not a claim that one format is more effective.

3. Approve a sample against a written specification

Ask whether the sample represents existing stock, an existing formula in reference packaging or a development version. Record the product, version and any requested changes.

Creatine gummy sample checklist

  • Match product identity and ingredient declaration to the supplied specification.
  • Review flavor, texture, appearance and count presentation.
  • Check bottle seal, closure, label readability and packaging fit.
  • Ask which product or batch documents are available and what they cover.
  • Record approval and any remaining questions before production.

A sensory sample review does not establish ingredient amount or replace relevant documents. Confirm sample quantity, fees and shipping using your destination country and postal code.

4. Select stock, own-label or custom development

Ready stock: bottled mixed orders start at 20 bottles total, with at least 3 bottles per product, using existing branding. Availability is confirmed in the quotation.

Your own label: customized bottled orders, including label-only branding, start at 500 bottles. An extra gift box or new container is optional. Discuss the existing bottle label route before commissioning new packaging.

Other formats or formula changes: confirm feasibility and MOQ separately. The 500-bottle rule should not automatically be applied to powder, pouches or every development project.

Ready stock ships 5–7 days after payment. Customized orders ship 15–20 days after sample approval. These are dispatch windows; shipping transit time is additional.

5. Send a short brief for a relevant quotation

Include your destination and sales channel, gummy or powder preference, reference product, branding route, estimated quantity and documents you need. If the formula is undecided, identify the requirements you want reviewed.

For the existing 80-count gummy reference, review bottle and shipping-carton specifications. Case packing quantity is not the same as the minimum order quantity.

Buyer questions

Does an 80-count bottle define the creatine serving?

No. Eighty gummies is the pack count. Request the current serving directions, gummies per serving and declared creatine amount for the exact product.

What is the minimum order for bottled creatine gummies?

Ready-stock mixed orders start at 20 bottles total, with at least 3 bottles per product. Own-label or other customized bottled orders start at 500 bottles. Pouches and powder formats need separate MOQ confirmation.

What should I compare in a gummy and powder quotation?

Compare the ingredient specification, declared amount per serving, serving presentation, complete blend, sensory attributes, packaging, available documents, quantity and shipping assumptions. Product names alone are not equivalent specifications.
