Private Label Shilajit Sourcing Guide: Resin vs Gummies

A practical B2B framework for comparing shilajit formats, specifications, samples, packaging and project routes without treating every product carrying the name as equivalent.

Published September 21, 2026•6-minute read•B2B sourcing guide

Short answer: Start by choosing the intended format, then request the exact material or formula specification, source and identity information available for that option, serving presentation, packaging, sample standard and destination-market requirements.

1. Choose the format before comparing quotations

Shilajit resin and shilajit gummies are different commercial systems. Resin uses a jar and direct-use format, while gummies involve a complete formula, gummy base, flavor system, serving presentation and bottle or pouch. The shared product name does not make the specifications interchangeable.

2. Request material identity and source information

Ask which material identity, source description and specification apply to the selected option. Avoid relying only on front-label words or unverified marketing descriptions. The information used for purchasing and label review should come from the proposed commercial product.

3. Compare the complete specification

For resin, review the composition, net-content direction, container and use presentation. For gummies, review the complete formula, proposed serving, gummy base, flavor, sweetener, color and packaging. Confirm all declared amounts from the selected specification.

4. Identify the commercial route

Three possible routes

  • Existing product: evaluate a current resin or gummy option.
  • Private label: use a selected product with brand-specific packaging.
  • Custom development: request separate review of material, formula, flavor, serving or packaging changes.

5. Use samples to confirm the selected version

Ask whether the sample represents current stock, an existing formula or a custom version. Record the sample identity and review appearance, texture or sensory characteristics, packaging function and consistency with the supplied specification.

6. Match packaging to the format

Resin projects may require a jar, closure, inner protection, label, carton and shipping protection. Gummy projects may use bottles or pouches with labels or printed components. Confirm packaging dimensions and availability before final artwork.

7. Request product-specific information

Ask which specifications, available quality or test documents and manufacturing-partner information apply to the selected product. Document availability, scope and destination-market relevance are confirmed by project.

8. Prepare a quotation brief

Include these details

  • Destination country and sales channel
  • Resin or gummy format
  • Existing, private-label or custom route
  • Required specification or formula attributes
  • Jar, bottle, pouch, label and carton direction
  • Estimated quantity and number of variants
  • Samples and documents needed

Frequently asked questions

Is resin automatically comparable with a gummy quotation?

No. Compare them as separate formats with different specifications, packaging systems, serving experiences and production requirements.

Can a shilajit product use custom packaging?

Packaging options can be discussed for selected projects. The available route and minimum quantity depend on the chosen product and printed components.
